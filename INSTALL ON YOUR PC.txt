GUARDORA - INSTALL ON YOUR PC
=============================

Use the PC that stays switched on the most. Guardora collects CVs while that PC is on,
and catches up on everything it missed (emails and website applications) when it is turned back on.

1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent, for example  C:\Guardora
   (not in Downloads, and not on a USB stick).

2. Double-click  "Install Guardora background.bat"
   - It installs Node.js if needed (click Yes if Windows asks), then Guardora.
   - Your browser opens at http://localhost:8787

3. Create your owner account (your name, email and a password of 10+ characters).

4. On the Overview page, click "Connect":
   - Website applications: the email and password you use at guardiansindia.com/admin
   - HR mailbox: follow the 3 steps on the screen to make a Gmail "app password" for hr@guardiansindia.com

5. Copy  data\master.key  to a USB stick or password manager and keep it safe.
   Without it, nobody (not even you) can open the stored CVs or backups.

Recommended on that PC: Settings > System > Power > Screen and sleep > "When plugged in, put my device to sleep" = Never.

Every day after that you just open http://localhost:8787 (or install it from Chrome's menu: "Install Guardora").

To stop Guardora completely: double-click "Remove Guardora background.bat" (your data is kept).
